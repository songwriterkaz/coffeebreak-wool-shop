Wool care guide

  • Wash less. Wool naturally resists odours. Airing a jumper overnight is often enough.
  • Hand wash cool. Use cool or lukewarm water with a wool wash. Gently squeeze, never wring or twist.
  • Machine wash only on a wool cycle if the label allows, inside a mesh bag, with a cold rinse.
  • No sudden temperature changes. Hot water and rubbing cause felting and shrinking.
  • Dry flat. Roll in a towel to remove water, then lay flat in shape, out of direct sun. Never tumble dry or hang wet knits.
  • Pilling is normal. Gently remove pills with a wool comb or fabric shaver.
  • Store folded, clean and dry, with cedar or lavender to keep moths away. Hangers stretch knits.
  • Baby blankets: wash before first use with a gentle, fragrance free wool wash.
